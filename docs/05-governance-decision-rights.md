# AI Faculty — Governance & Decision Rights Model

**Version:** v0.1 (synthesised from [missions/mission-006-founder-response.md](missions/mission-006-founder-response.md))
**Status:** first draft

---

## 1. Purpose

Make explicit **who decides what**, **on what evidence**, and **how a decision can be challenged** —
so the institution can grow and delegate without losing human authority or quality.

## 2. Decision classes

| # | Class | Owner | Evidence requirement | Reversible? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Constitutional / Mission** | Founder + (future) human governance body — *reserved* | Explicit ratified amendment | Only by further amendment |
| 2 | **Safety / Risk** | Quality & Safety Faculty | Precautionary default under uncertainty | Yes, once evidence resolves |
| 3 | **Institutional architecture** (model swap, new control-plane service, new decision class) | Founder approves | Evidence-backed; Research + Quality review | Yes, with staged rollback |
| 4 | **Curriculum — core** | Founder approves entry to core | Research Confidence ≥ R3; defined outcome + rubric + prerequisites + faculty competence | Yes (version forward / retire) |
| 4e | **Curriculum — experimental elective / research track** | Learning Architect + Research Faculty | R1–R2, clearly labelled as fast-moving | Yes |
| 5 | **Assessment / mastery decision** | Authorised Assessment Faculty holder, via the Assessment Resolution Protocol (§5) | Rubric + multi-assessor evidence | Appeal path (§6) |
| 6 | **Learner-level pedagogical decisions** | Pathway / Teaching engines + relevant Faculty | Learner Intelligence Model state | Continuously |
| 7 | **Personnel / progression** on the Faculty Growth Ladder | The authority one level above the target role; Founder approves *first-of-kind* entries | Verified capability + reliability + judgement + behaviour + performance | Yes (probation, calibration tracking) |
| 8 | **Compensation** | Founder approves the framework and each paid arrangement | Qualified work performed | Yes |

## 3. Decision-rights principles

1. Every decision records: **owner class · evidence · rationale · review/appeal path · reversibility note.**
2. **Blind-first, evidence-based resolution** for every disagreement (§5).
3. **Authority follows demonstrated expertise + role, not seniority.** Higher authority ⇒ more audit.
4. The **AI never holds unilateral authority** over safety, mastery, curriculum, or architecture —
   it proposes and evidences; humans / authorised roles decide.
5. **Precautionary default** under uncertainty for anything with harm potential.
6. The Founder can **always tighten** safety/quality; **loosening** requires evidence + Quality &
   Safety sign-off.
7. **Reserved Founder powers:** the Constitution, the mission, protection of human authority, and
   first-of-kind approvals. Each is subject to a periodic *"does this still need the Founder, or has
   trusted expertise emerged to delegate it?"* review, and delegated only by explicit recorded decision.

## 4. Tie-break / escalation ladder

```
automated check
   → relevant Faculty
   → specialist Faculty
   → Quality & Safety Faculty (safety)  /  Research Faculty (matter of fact)
   → human authority (Founder)
   → (future) human governance body
```

Constitutional / mission matters skip straight to reserved powers.

## 5. Assessment Resolution Protocol (v0.1)

For any mastery decision or contested assessment:

1. **Blind independent assessment** — each qualified assessor scores the evidence without seeing
   other scores (no anchoring).
2. **Divergence detection** — score variance, rubric disagreement, conflicting reasoning, missing
   evidence, safety concerns, confidence differences.
3. **Reasoned review** — assessors see the relevant *reasoning and evidence* (not "everyone else
   gave 90") and respond *Agree* / *Disagree — because…*
4. **Reassessment** — assessors may revise. Changing a score is **not** failure; it can be evidence
   of good reasoning.
5. **Specialist escalation** — if meaningful disagreement remains, or the case exceeds the
   assessors' authority.
6. **Final decision** — the authorised Faculty holder decides.

Rounds stay partially blind to avoid groupthink. **Disagreement is an investigation signal.** The
protocol doubles as a learning process for the assessors (their misses become their own evidence).

## 6. Appeal

A learner or a dissenting assessor may request one re-run of the protocol with a fresh assessor set
and a specialist observer. The outcome and reasoning are recorded either way.

## 7. Payment firewall

Compensation is for **qualified work performed**. It must **never** influence an assessment outcome.
Paid assessors are subject to the same calibration tracking and audit as unpaid ones — more, not
less.

## 8. Model-change rule (class 3 detail)

New models are adopted only after: parallel shadow evaluation on our own rubrics + safety +
calibration + regression on fixed past tasks → Quality & Safety sign-off → staged rollout
(Research → assessors → teaching → learners) with rollback → prior model pinned for reproducibility.
