# AI Faculty — Knowledge & Research Protocol

**Version:** v0.1
**Why it exists:** AI changes faster than a conventional curriculum-development cycle. The
institution needs a permanent mechanism to keep what it teaches true and current.

---

## The knowledge lifecycle

```
discover → verify → evaluate → teach → test → update → version → retire
```

Nothing is deleted on retirement — it is versioned out and kept in history.

## Multi-source research protocol

No important institutional decision rests on one AI's opinion. For significant decisions, synthesise:

```
primary sources + academic literature + industry evidence + standards/governance
+ multiple AI systems + human experts + contradictory evidence
      ↓
   synthesis → confidence assessment → decision
```

**Different AI models are not independent truth sources merely because they are different models.**
Five models echoing one unsupported claim is one claim echoed five times, not five pieces of evidence.

## Epistemic hierarchy

| Level | Question |
|---|---|
| Evidence | What actually supports the proposition? |
| Interpretation | What do researchers / experts think it means? |
| Model synthesis | What are AI systems suggesting? |
| Institutional conclusion | What does the AI Faculty currently believe? |

## Research Confidence Levels

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **R0** | Unverified claim |
| **R1** | AI / model suggestion |
| **R2** | Multiple-source support |
| **R3** | Strong evidence / expert corroboration |
| **R4** | Institutionally validated |
| **R5** | Repeatedly validated / research-grade confidence |

**R5 is not eternal truth** — it is the strongest evidence accumulated so far. The institution must
remain able to change its mind (Constitution §8).

Curriculum-entry gate: **core** curriculum requires ≥ R3; **experimental electives** may run at R1–R2
with clear labelling (see governance decision class 4 / 4e).
