# AI Faculty — Faculty Growth & Compensation

**Version:** v0.1

---

## The Faculty Growth Ladder

```
Learner → Capable Learner → Mastered Learner → Peer Mentor → Peer Reviewer
→ Specialist Reviewer → Junior Faculty → Specialist Faculty → Senior Faculty
→ Research / Innovation Lead
```

- Advancement is by **verified capability + reliability + judgement + appropriate behaviour +
  demonstrated performance** — **not time served**.
- Each step up **increases** the monitoring, review and audit the holder receives.
- Authority follows demonstrated expertise + institutional role, not seniority.
- Teaching or assessing someone else generates evidence of the mentor's *own* mastery.

## Risk guard

Progression must never mean "I am higher ranking, therefore I am right." Higher responsibility =
higher accountability. Senior learners do not gain power *over* junior learners; they gain
obligations *to* them.

## Scoped roles for learners

| Learner role | May do | May not do |
|---|---|---|
| Peer Mentor | Mentor, explain, encourage, give formative feedback | Score official assessments |
| Peer Reviewer | Formative review of artefacts against a rubric | Make mastery decisions |
| Specialist Reviewer | Contribute blind scores to the multi-assessor pool (in mastered subjects), under supervision | Make the final mastery decision |

All learner assessment work is shadowed by an authorised Assessment Faculty holder during a
probation period, with calibration tracking.

## Compensation framework (future mechanism — not yet active)

`Learn → Contribute → Earn → Lead → Research → Create`

| Stage | Possible arrangement |
|---|---|
| Learner | Education is the activity; no payment |
| Mentor | Recognition / credits; compensation where appropriate |
| Specialist reviewer | Paid compensation for approved assessment work |
| Faculty | Defined remuneration arrangement |
| Research contributor | Compensation, grants, royalties, employment — depending on the work |

### Hard rules

1. **Payment must never buy a passing grade.** No one is ever financially incentivised toward a
   particular assessment outcome.
2. Compensation is for **qualified work performed**; assessment decisions stay governed by evidence
   and institutional rules.
3. Paid contributors receive **more** audit, not less.
4. The Founder approves the framework and each individual paid arrangement (decision class 8).

## Talent & Leadership Engine

The institution is also a talent-discovery engine. It watches for demonstrated strength in:
technical reasoning · research · teaching · assessment · systems thinking · creativity · leadership ·
safety judgement · scientific thinking · software engineering · communication — and routes those
learners toward opportunities. Someone who enters as a beginner may one day teach the Founder
something new. That is a feature.
