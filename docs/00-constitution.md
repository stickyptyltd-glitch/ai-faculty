# AI Faculty — Constitution

**Version:** v0.1 (seeded from the ChatGPT thread, 2026-09-01)
**Status:** ratified in concept; open to amendment via §11.

---

## 1. Purpose

The AI Faculty exists to help people develop useful, durable and transferable capability in
artificial intelligence — through excellent teaching, guided practice, rigorous assessment, visible
progress, and continuously updated knowledge.

## 2. The learner comes first

The system optimises for **capability gained**, not time spent, content consumed, messages exchanged,
or badges collected.

## 3. Human authority

AI may teach, coach, analyse, generate and recommend. Human oversight retains responsibility for:
institutional direction, quality standards, consequential decisions, safety, unresolved disputes,
and changes to this Constitution. The AI never holds unilateral authority over safety, mastery
decisions, curriculum, or institutional architecture.

## 4. Truth before fluency

The Faculty must prefer a qualified uncertainty, or a request for verification, over a confident
unsupported answer. Important claims must carry appropriate evidence and currency.

## 5. Mastery before progression

Completion is not mastery. Advancement depends on demonstrated capability appropriate to the
learning outcome.

## 6. Enjoyment is part of quality

Learning should be intellectually stimulating and varied. A boring institution is a lower-quality
institution.

## 7. AI literacy, including limitations

Learners are taught what AI cannot reliably do, and how to verify it, not only how to use it.

## 8. Continuous evolution

Curriculum, faculty and platform improve as AI itself changes. No design is treated as final.
**Never overwrite — version forward.** Every change is recorded in the Continuity Log.

## 9. Accessibility and respect

The institution is designed to be usable by people with limited resources (it began on a phone) and
treats every learner with respect regardless of starting level.

## 10. The non-negotiable quality gate

Growth can be extremely fast. **Untested assumptions cannot.** Every phase has an explicit quality
gate. Nothing scales until the thing being scaled is proven at the current level.

Operating cycle: **Plan → Build → Test → Measure → Improve → Document → Scale → Repeat.**

## 11. Amendment

This Constitution is changed only by an explicit, recorded amendment approved under the reserved
powers defined in [05-governance-decision-rights.md](05-governance-decision-rights.md). Amendments
are versioned; superseded text is retained in history, not deleted.

## 12. Authority follows expertise

Institutional authority follows demonstrated expertise and institutional role — **not organisational
seniority alone**. The more authority a person or system holds, the more monitoring, review and audit
they receive. Progression is never a licence to be presumed right.
